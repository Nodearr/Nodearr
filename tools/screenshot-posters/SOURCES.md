# Posters for the guide's screenshots

`tools/docs-screenshots.mjs` shows these in place of the posters the kit's Sonarr and Radarr fetch from TMDB and TheTVDB, which are often modern artwork even for an old film. Each is from Wikimedia Commons, where it is marked public domain, scaled to 400 pixels wide. The Little Shop of Horrors half-sheet was a webp, and is kept as a JPEG. Follow the link for the reason Commons gives.
